Lottery versus stride
GOAL: Compare random selection with deterministic pass-based selection under the same workload.
CAUSE: tickets -> policy -> selection -> CPU
DATA: proc.tickets | proc.pass | proc.state | proc.ticks
CORRECTION: same work -> two policies -> same units -> fair comparison
INTERVIEWER TRAP: random mean -> stride bound -> short interval -> wrong claim
MANUAL CALCULATION: For 30:20:10 tickets, ideal shares are 1/2,1/3,1/6. A ten-quantum sample need not match those fractions.
EVIDENCE STATUS: Published stride short-window counterexample; not an identified xv6 defect.
PRIMARY/RELEVANT SOURCE: https://publications.csail.mit.edu/lcs/pubs/pdf/MIT-LCS-TR-667.pdf
