The compiled C drills isolate mechanisms. They do not replace upstream xv6, JOS, Pintos, KVM, or libvirt source.
Use: cd assignments/14-mit-61810-cow; make status; make check (expected to fail until your logic is implemented); bash bootstrap.sh for the full university repo.
All tests in logic_drill.c are intentionally RED. Do not claim a green build is a green university solution.
For a real project integration: inspect README.md and CHALLENGES.md, implement the ten tasks in the official source, run original tests.
For KVM host experiments require /dev/kvm; for xv6 labs require the expected cross compiler and QEMU; for libvirt require hypervisor and guests.
