B05 | KSM and automatic NUMA balancing cause long guest-wide stalls
PUBLIC ORIGIN YEAR: 2024
EVIDENCE STATUS: CONFIRMED_REPORT_BISECTED_PATCH_VALIDATED
CAN TRY ON SECOND LAPTOP: NEEDS_LARGE_DUAL_NUMA_HOST
LOCAL REPRODUCED: NO_REPRO_RUN
CORE FAILURE, NOT AN ERROR-RETURN EXERCISE:
KVM TDP MMU page-fault handling contended on mmu_lock during MMU notifier invalidation. Fault retried while invalidation active without waiting outside lock could amplify latency. Patch prechecks retry before mmu_lock.
OBSERVED OPERATION THAT MATTERS:
Reporter used 256 GiB host/2 NUMA nodes, 140 GiB guest, KSM pages_to_scan=1250, 128 GiB identical memory, then 32 processes ×4 GiB, NUMA balancing enabled.
FUNCTION-TO-FUNCTION REASONING PATH:
host NUMA balance migrates pages -> MMU notifier invalidates -> vCPU faults -> mmu_lock contention -> guest cannot make progress while task_numa_work stalls; retry earlier -> avoids contention
ORIGINAL REPORT / PATCH (READ BODY NOT TITLE): https://lists.openwall.net/linux-kernel/2024/01/04/582
FIX OR UNCERTAINTY: https://lists.openwall.net/linux-kernel/2024/02/14/687
REPRODUCTION PROCEDURE AND REQUIRED RESOURCES:
Requires large NUMA lab, original QEMU 8.2 recipe, KSM enabled, and 140 GiB guest. Not reproducible as specified on a 14 GiB single-NUMA laptop. Do not scale down and claim equivalent failure.
MEANINGFUL SUCCESS/FAILURE SIGNAL:
reporter saw 1-100s guest stalls; experiment showed disappearance with NUMA balancing off and with v4 patch
EXACT UNIVERSITY ASSIGNMENTS INVOLVED: 08-iitb-cs695-kvm-pa1; 17-pintos-cs318-vm; 19-uiuc-cs423-mp3; 07-gatech-6210-vm-coordinator
